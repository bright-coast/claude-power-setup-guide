How does an email get from you to someone else?

A picture guide in nine steps.

  • Plain English
  • About two minutes
  1. 1

    Analogy, not the real thing

    Email is a bit like posting a letter.

    Same idea. Much faster.

  2. 2

    An email is a message with an address on it.

  3. 3

    You press Send. Your email service picks it up.

    Gmail and Outlook are examples.

  4. 4

    The part after the @ says where it goes.

    Your service looks it up.

  5. 5

    Your service contacts theirs across the internet.

    Both speak the same agreed language.

  6. 6

    Their service checks it is real, not junk.

    Most junk gets stopped here.

  7. 7

    It waits safely in their mailbox.

    Until they next check their email.

  8. 8

    They open their email, and it appears.

    Their app shows it in the inbox.

  9. 9

    The whole trip usually takes seconds.

    If it fails, you get a message back.

This is a simplified picture, made to be understood rather than to be complete.
